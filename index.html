(03) Process

From first spark to full flame.

  1. 01 / Ignite

    Discover

    A deep-dive call on your goals, audience and competitors. We find the one idea your site should stand on.

  2. 02 / Shape

    Design

    Sitemap, wireframes and a visual direction you can react to. You see and approve the design before a line of code.

  3. 03 / Build

    Develop

    Pixel-true, responsive at every screen size, fast to load and accessible by default.

  4. 04 / Launch

    Go live

    Testing, launch and a clean handover — with support after go-live so the site keeps working for you.
